# Madhava 1.34

Motion, kept purposeful and short (all of it switches off when the phone's "reduce motion" setting is on):

- Thinking indicator: three marigold dots replace the italic "Krishna is thinking" text.
- Interactive explanation cards: ease in when they arrive, collapse smoothly when minimized, and fade out when closed.
- Screens fade and rise slightly when switching between Home, Chat, Settings and Study.
- Calls: a soft golden glow behind Krishna pulses while he speaks.
- The newest message (and card) fades in; older messages never re-animate.

# Gita grounding sources

701 Sanskrit verse records across 18 chapters, plus 313 English passage chunks.

Sanskrit: https://github.com/vedicscriptures/bhagavad-gita — original ancient Sanskrit and transliteration only; no modern translations or commentaries copied. Source numbering is preserved, including the additional opening verse in chapter 13 in this edition.

English: Edwin Arnold, The Song Celestial (1885), Project Gutenberg ebook 2388. Public-domain poetic rendering. Complete downloaded source and its Gutenberg license are retained. English passages carry chapter references only, never invented verse alignment.

Retrieval: local lexical ranking, exact chapter/verse lookup and a hand-authored multilingual topic index. No embedding service. Topic notes are short original paraphrases to help retrieval, not literal translations. Model-generated explanations remain fallible.

Welcome to Madhava

A devotional space for conversation and study

Krishna and the other forms are AI portrayals inspired by devotional traditions. Responses can make mistakes. Source passages and AI interpretations are shown separately.

Text and recent conversation context go to Google Gemini when you chat. Calls send microphone audio; camera images are sent only when you enable camera sharing. Images you attach and voice you dictate are sent to Gemini only when you use those buttons. Google’s service terms and data handling apply to your API key.

Offline scripture reading and 3D previews do not need a key. This beta has no account or subscription.

Privacy and your data

Your API key is encrypted in Android Keystore on this device. Chats, call transcripts, bookmarks, private reflections, preferences and report drafts are stored locally. In a browser preview, the key is kept in memory.

Google Gemini receives your messages and recent conversation context. Images you attach and recordings you dictate are sent to Gemini for that one request. During calls it receives microphone audio and, only while enabled, camera frames. The app does not save raw audio or video recordings; a small thumbnail of an attached image stays in your chat history on this device.

Clear history in Settings to remove chats and call transcripts. Delete all local data also removes your saved key, bookmarks, preferences and report drafts. This does not delete data already processed by Google.

Reports in this beta are local drafts, not delivered to a reviewer. A public privacy policy, operator contact and working report service are required before public release.

# Madhava 1.33

- Krishna now remembers the interactive explanation he showed. Each card carries a short summary written by Krishna himself (or text recovered from the card), which is added to the conversation sent to Gemini, in chat and when seeding calls, so he can explain or answer questions about the card later.
- Interactive explanation cards have explicit buttons: minimize, expand to full screen, and close. A closed card leaves a small "Show the interactive explanation" chip that reopens it; the choice is remembered. In calls, close removes the overlay and minimize leaves a slim bar so the 3D model stays visible.
- Dev tools: tools/try-prompt.mjs (try a prompt with your own key) and a sample Kathopanishad widget.
